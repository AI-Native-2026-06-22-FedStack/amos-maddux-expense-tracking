## 1. Purpose and Scope `NWP-POL-005-01`

This policy governs reimbursement for meals, event tickets, and
entertainment **when a client, prospect, or other external business
party is present.** An employee's own meals while traveling alone are
governed by `NWP-POL-004` (Employee Meals Policy), not this document.

## 2. Client Entertainment Per-Person Caps `NWP-POL-005-02`

**Material difference from NWP-POL-004-03:** this section covers
client-facing meals/entertainment and permits materially higher
per-person caps than the solo-employee meal caps in `NWP-POL-004-03`,
because client entertainment additionally requires recording the
client's name, company, and business purpose in the expense report — a
documentation requirement `NWP-POL-004-03` does not carry.

| Event type | Cap (USD/person) |
| --- | --- |
| Client meal (breakfast/lunch) | $60 |
| Client meal (dinner) | $110 |
| Client event/tickets (sporting, theater, etc.) | $150 |

Caps apply per person, per event, inclusive of tax and gratuity. A
moderate amount of alcohol is reimbursable under this policy when
consumed with a client meal, unlike under `NWP-POL-004`.

### 2.1 Required Documentation `NWP-POL-005-02.01`

Every client entertainment expense must record, in the expense report
notes field: the client's full name, the client's company, and a
one-sentence business purpose. Claims missing any of these three fields
are returned to the submitting employee for correction before approval.

## 3. Gifts `NWP-POL-005-03`

Client gifts are reimbursable up to $75 per recipient per calendar year,
and must comply with the recipient's own organization's gift-acceptance
policy where known.
