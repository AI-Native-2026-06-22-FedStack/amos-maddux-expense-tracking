## 1. Purpose and Scope `NWP-POL-004-01`

This policy sets reimbursement caps for an employee's own meals while
traveling on approved business activity, **when no client, prospect, or
other external party is present.** Meals involving a client or prospect
are governed by `NWP-POL-005` (Client Entertainment Policy), not this
document.

## 2. General Rules `NWP-POL-004-02`

Meal caps apply per person, per day, and are inclusive of tax and
gratuity. Alcohol is not reimbursable under this policy. Meals are only
reimbursable on days that include overnight travel or travel exceeding
50 miles from the employee's regular worksite.

## 3. Standard Meal Caps `NWP-POL-004-03`

**Material difference from NWP-POL-005-02:** this section covers an
employee's own solo meals while traveling (no client present) and uses
materially lower per-person caps with no documentation requirement beyond
a receipt; `NWP-POL-005-02` covers client-facing entertainment, uses
higher caps, and additionally requires recording the client's name and
business purpose.

| Meal | Cap (USD/person/day) |
| --- | --- |
| Breakfast | $18 |
| Lunch | $22 |
| Dinner | $45 |

### 3.1 Receipt Requirement `NWP-POL-004-03.01`

Receipts are required for any single meal above the general receipt
threshold set in `NWP-POL-006-01`. Below that threshold, a per-diem style
self-attestation is sufficient.
