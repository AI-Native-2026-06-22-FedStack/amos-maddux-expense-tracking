## 1. Purpose and Scope `NWP-POL-012-01`

This policy sets expense report submission deadlines and describes the
post-approval audit sampling process for Northwind Prairie Holdings,
Inc. It also records the history of the submission-deadline rule itself,
since that rule has changed since this corpus's earliest synthetic
policy version.

## 2. Expense Report Submission Deadline — Legacy `NWP-POL-012-02`

**Status: SUPERSEDED — see NWP-POL-012-05.**

Effective date: 2019-01-01. Superseded: 2024-06-01.

Employees must submit expense reports within **60 calendar days** of the
date the expense was incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days require
`Finance Admin` approval to process and may be denied at `Finance
Admin` discretion.

This rule is retained here for historical reference only. It no longer
governs submissions made on or after 2024-06-01; see the current rule at
`NWP-POL-012-05`.

### 2.1 Late-Submission Handling Under the Legacy Rule `NWP-POL-012-02.01`

Under the legacy 60-day rule, a report submitted between 60 and 90 days
after the expense date required `Finance Admin` approval; a report
submitted after 90 days was denied outright with no exception path. This
subsection is retained for historical reference only alongside the
superseded rule above.

## 3. Audit Sampling Rate by Report-Value Tier `NWP-POL-012-03`

| Report value tier | Sampling rate |
| --- | --- |
| Under $500 | 2% of reports sampled |
| $500 – $5,000 | 10% of reports sampled |
| Above $5,000 | 100% of reports sampled |

Sampled reports are reviewed by `Finance Admin` staff not involved in
the original approval, per the segregation-of-duties expectation
described in Section 4.

## 4. Segregation of Duties `NWP-POL-012-04`

The `Finance Admin` who conducts an audit sampling review of a report
must not be the same `Finance Admin` who approved that report, when
staffing allows. Where only one `Finance Admin` is available, the review
is escalated to a second reviewing entity outside the normal approval
chain.

## 5. Expense Report Submission Deadline — Current `NWP-POL-012-05`

**Status: CURRENT — replaces NWP-POL-012-02 effective 2024-06-01.**

Effective date: 2024-06-01.

Employees must submit expense reports within **30 calendar days** of the
date the expense was incurred. This rule supersedes the prior 60-day
deadline in `NWP-POL-012-02` effective 2024-06-01. Reports submitted
after 30 days require `Department Manager` approval to process; reports
submitted after 60 days additionally require `Finance Admin` approval
and may be denied at `Finance Admin` discretion.

The deadline was shortened from 60 to 30 days to align expense
recognition more closely with the monthly close process described
elsewhere in Northwind Prairie Holdings, Inc.'s (fictional) internal
finance calendar.
