## 1. Purpose and Scope `NWP-POL-001-01`

This policy governs reimbursement for ground travel undertaken by
employees of Northwind Prairie Holdings, Inc. in the course of approved
business activity, including personal-vehicle mileage, rental vehicles,
rideshare, taxi, parking, and tolls. It applies to all employees in the
`Employee`, `Department Manager`, and `Finance Admin` roles.

### 1.1 Applicability `NWP-POL-001-01.01`

This policy applies to ground travel within the employee's home country
only. International ground travel is governed by
`NWP-POL-011` (International Travel Policy).

## 2. Personal Vehicle Mileage Reimbursement `NWP-POL-001-02`

**Material difference from NWP-POL-010-02:** this section governs
per-mile reimbursement for routine business travel; `NWP-POL-010-02`
governs a one-time relocation moving-distance reimbursement and uses a
flat tier structure rather than a per-mile rate.

Employees who use a personal vehicle for approved business travel are
reimbursed per mile driven, at a rate that varies by vehicle class. The
standard mileage rate is reviewed annually and may be adjusted to track
published fuel-cost indices.

| Vehicle class                             | Rate (USD/mile) | Notes                                              |
| ----------------------------------------- | --------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Standard passenger vehicle                | $0.67           | Default rate for sedans, hatchbacks, standard SUVs |
| Hybrid or electric vehicle                | $0.70           | Incentive rate to encourage lower-emission travel  |
| Motorcycle                                | $0.35           |                                                    |
| Personal vehicle towing equipment/trailer | $0.75           | Requires prior `Department Manager` approval       |

Mileage is calculated from the employee's regular worksite to the
business destination, net of the employee's normal commute distance.
Commute-distance deduction does not apply on weekends or company
holidays.

### 2.1 Odometer and Mapping Evidence `NWP-POL-001-02.01`

Mileage claims must be supported by either odometer readings at
departure and arrival, or a mapping-service (routed-distance) printout.
Estimated or rounded mileage without supporting evidence is not
reimbursable.

## 3. Rental Vehicles `NWP-POL-001-03`

Rental vehicles may be used in place of personal-vehicle mileage when
total round-trip mileage exceeds 150 miles, or when a personal vehicle is
unavailable. Rentals must be booked in the economy or standard class
unless three or more employees are traveling together, in which case a
mid-size or larger vehicle may be booked.

### 3.1 Insurance `NWP-POL-001-03.01`

Employees must decline the rental company's supplemental insurance for
domestic travel, since Northwind Prairie Holdings, Inc. carries a
corporate policy covering rental vehicles used for business purposes.

## 4. Rideshare, Taxi, Parking, and Tolls `NWP-POL-001-04`

Rideshare and taxi fares are reimbursable for business travel when more
cost-effective than a rental vehicle or when driving is impractical.
Parking and toll charges incurred during approved business travel are
fully reimbursable with receipt, regardless of amount, per the
documentation thresholds in `NWP-POL-006`.
