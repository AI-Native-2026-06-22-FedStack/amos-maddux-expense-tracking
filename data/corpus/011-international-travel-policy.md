## 1. Purpose and Scope `NWP-POL-011-01`

This policy governs business travel outside the employee's home country,
including per-diem rates, currency handling, and visa/vaccination cost
reimbursement. Domestic ground and air travel remain governed by
`NWP-POL-001` and `NWP-POL-002`.

## 2. International Per Diem by Region Tier `NWP-POL-011-02`

Per diem covers meals and incidentals only; lodging is booked and
reimbursed separately per `NWP-POL-003`, adjusted for international
market cost at `Finance Admin` discretion.

### 2.1 Currency and Conversion `NWP-POL-011-02.01`

Per diem is paid in USD. Out-of-pocket foreign-currency expenses are
converted to USD using the conversion rate in effect on the transaction
date, as reflected on the employee's card statement or a published daily
rate if paid in cash.

## 3. Visa and Vaccination Costs `NWP-POL-011-03`

Visa application fees and business-required vaccinations are fully
reimbursable with receipt when required for an approved international
trip. Passport renewal costs are not reimbursable, since a valid
passport is a standing employee responsibility, not a per-trip cost.

## 4. Approval `NWP-POL-011-04`

All international travel spend above $5,000 requires the dual sign-off
described in `NWP-POL-007-03`.
