## 1. Eligibility `NWP-POL-010-01`

Relocation reimbursement is available to an employee who accepts a
company-initiated transfer, or a new hire whose offer letter explicitly
includes relocation assistance. Voluntary relocations not tied to a
company-initiated transfer are not covered by this policy.

## 2. Relocation Reimbursement Caps by Move Distance `NWP-POL-010-02`

**Material difference from NWP-POL-001-02:** this section sets a
flat one-time reimbursement tier for a full household relocation, unlike
`NWP-POL-001-02`, which reimburses routine business travel at a per-mile
rate rather than a flat move-distance tier.

| Move distance       | Reimbursement cap (USD) |
| ------------------- | ----------------------- |
| 50–199 miles        | $3,000                  |
| 200–999 miles       | $7,500                  |
| 1,000 miles or more | $12,000                 |

Caps cover moving-company or truck-rental costs, temporary lodging up to
14 nights, and standard packing materials. They do not cover real estate
commissions or lease-break penalties.

### 2.1 Repayment on Early Departure `NWP-POL-010-02.01`

An employee who voluntarily resigns within 12 months of a
company-funded relocation must repay the relocation reimbursement on a
prorated basis, per the repayment schedule in the employee's relocation
agreement.

## 3. Approval `NWP-POL-010-03`

All relocation reimbursement requires the dual sign-off described in
`NWP-POL-007-03`, regardless of amount, due to the size and one-time
nature of relocation spend.
