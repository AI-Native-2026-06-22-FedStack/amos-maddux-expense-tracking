## 1. Receipt Required Threshold — General `NWP-POL-006-01`

**Material difference from NWP-POL-006-04:** this section sets the
receipt-required threshold for **out-of-pocket / reimbursement**
expenses; `NWP-POL-006-04` sets a materially **higher** threshold for
**corporate card** transactions, because corporate-card spend is already
captured in the monthly statement feed described in `NWP-POL-008`.

A receipt is required for any out-of-pocket expense above the following
per-category thresholds. Expenses at or below the threshold may be
submitted with a self-attestation in place of a receipt.

| Category | Receipt required above (USD) |
| --- | --- |
| Meals | $25 |
| Ground transportation | $25 |
| Lodging | $0 (always required) |
| Office/general supplies | $25 |
| Other | $25 |

### 1.1 Acceptable Receipt Formats `NWP-POL-006-01.01`

Acceptable formats are a photographed or scanned itemized receipt, an
emailed vendor invoice, or a vendor-issued digital receipt. A credit card
statement line alone is not an acceptable substitute for a receipt.

## 2. Missing Receipt Affidavit `NWP-POL-006-02`

When a required receipt is lost, the employee may submit a missing
receipt affidavit describing the expense, in lieu of the receipt, no
more than twice per rolling 12-month period. A third or subsequent
missing-receipt affidavit in the same period requires `Department
Manager` approval.

## 3. Retention `NWP-POL-006-03`

Original receipts (paper or digital) should be retained by the employee
for 90 days after report approval, in case of an audit sampling review
under `NWP-POL-012-03`.
