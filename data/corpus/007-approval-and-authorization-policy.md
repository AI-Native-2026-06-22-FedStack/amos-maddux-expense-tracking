## 1. Standard Approval Thresholds `NWP-POL-007-01`

**Material difference from NWP-POL-007-03:** this section sets the
approval chain for ordinary domestic business expense, and its top tier
requires only a single `Finance Admin` sign-off; `NWP-POL-007-03` covers
international travel and relocation spend and requires **dual** sign-off
(`Department Manager` AND `Finance Admin`) at the equivalent top tier,
reflecting the higher risk of that spend category.

| Report total     | Required approval                          |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Up to $500       | `Department Manager`                       |
| $500.01 – $5,000 | `Department Manager`, then `Finance Admin` |

### 1.1 Self-Approval Prohibition `NWP-POL-007-01.01`

No employee may approve their own expense report, regardless of role or
report total. A report submitted by a `Department Manager` routes to
another `Department Manager` or directly to `Finance Admin`.

## 2. Delegation `NWP-POL-007-02`

A `Department Manager` may delegate approval authority to another
`Department Manager` for up to 14 consecutive calendar days, for example
during planned leave. Delegation must be recorded in writing to
`Finance Admin` before it takes effect.
