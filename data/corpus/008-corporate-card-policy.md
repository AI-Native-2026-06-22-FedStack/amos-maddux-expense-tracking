## 1. Eligibility and Issuance `NWP-POL-008-01`

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel on approved business
activity at least quarterly, or upon `Department Manager` request for an
employee with recurring business purchasing needs. Cards are issued in
the employee's own name and are not transferable.

### 1.1 Application Process `NWP-POL-008-01.01`

Card requests are submitted through the internal request portal at
`intranet.northwindprairie.example/cards` and require `Department
Manager` approval before issuance.

## 2. Permitted and Prohibited Use `NWP-POL-008-02`

Corporate cards may be used only for business expenses covered by this
corpus's other policies (travel, lodging, meals, client entertainment,
office supplies). Personal purchases of any kind, including incidental
personal purchases intended for later reimbursement to the company, are
prohibited on a corporate card.

## 3. Statement Reconciliation `NWP-POL-008-03`

Cardholders must reconcile all corporate card transactions in the
expense system within 15 calendar days of the monthly statement close.
Unreconciled transactions after 45 days trigger an automatic notice to
the cardholder's `Department Manager`.

## 4. Misuse and Card Revocation `NWP-POL-008-04`

Personal use of a corporate card, even if reimbursed to the company, is
treated as a policy violation and may result in card revocation.
Repeated late reconciliation (three or more late statements in a
rolling 12-month period) may also result in card revocation at
`Finance Admin` discretion.
