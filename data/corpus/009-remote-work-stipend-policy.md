## 1. Purpose and Eligibility `NWP-POL-009-01`

This policy provides a recurring stipend to employees of Northwind
Prairie Holdings, Inc. who are designated fully remote or hybrid (three
or fewer in-office days per week) by their `Department Manager`. This
policy does not apply to occasional work-from-home days by an otherwise
office-based employee.

## 2. Stipend Caps by Category `NWP-POL-009-02`

| Category                                     | Cap  | Frequency                                    |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Home office equipment (desk, chair, monitor) | $500 | One-time, per employee, resets every 3 years |
| Internet service                             | $50  | Monthly                                      |
| Utilities (electricity/heating offset)       | $30  | Monthly                                      |

### 2.1 Equipment Ownership `NWP-POL-009-02.01`

Equipment purchased under the one-time equipment cap remains company
property and must be returned or its depreciated value reimbursed upon
termination of employment, per the return process in the employee
handbook (outside the scope of this corpus).

## 3. Claim Process `NWP-POL-009-03`

Monthly stipend amounts (internet, utilities) are claimed through the
standard expense report process and do not require a receipt below the
general threshold set in `NWP-POL-006-01`. The one-time equipment cap
always requires an itemized receipt regardless of amount.
