## 1. Purpose and Scope `NWP-POL-003-01`

This policy sets nightly lodging caps for employees of Northwind Prairie
Holdings, Inc. traveling on approved business activity. Caps vary by
city tier to reflect real differences in market lodging cost.

## 2. Nightly Lodging Caps by City Tier `NWP-POL-003-02`

| City tier | Example markets (synthetic) | Nightly cap (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 (major metro) | Fictional cities: Prairiehaven, Lake Meridian | $320 |
| Tier 2 (secondary metro) | Fictional cities: Cedarbrook, North Elston | $220 |
| Tier 3 (all other locations) | All other synthetic locations | $150 |

Nightly caps are inclusive of room rate and mandatory hotel fees, but
exclusive of taxes, which are separately reimbursable.

### 2.1 Exceeding the Cap `NWP-POL-003-02.01`

Bookings exceeding the applicable nightly cap require prior written
approval from a `Department Manager`. Conference-hosted-hotel bookings
that exceed the cap are pre-approved automatically when the hotel is the
official conference venue and the excess is under $40/night.

## 3. Extended Stays `NWP-POL-003-03`

For stays of 14 consecutive nights or longer at one location, employees
should book extended-stay or corporate-rate lodging where available,
which is often below the standard nightly cap. Extended stays remain
subject to the Tier cap in Section 2 unless a `Finance Admin` approves an
extended-stay exception in advance.
