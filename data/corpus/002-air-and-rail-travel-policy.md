## 1. Purpose and Scope `NWP-POL-002-01`

This policy governs booked air and rail travel for employees of
Northwind Prairie Holdings, Inc. traveling on approved business activity
within their home country. International air travel booking follows this
policy's class-of-service rules together with the currency and visa
guidance in `NWP-POL-011` (International Travel Policy).

## 2. Class of Service `NWP-POL-002-02`

Class of service is determined by flight duration and, separately, by how
far in advance the trip is booked.

| Flight duration | Standard booking (14+ days advance) | Late booking (< 14 days advance)              |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Under 3 hours   | Economy                             | Economy                                       |
| 3 to 6 hours    | Economy                             | Economy Plus / Premium Economy                |
| Over 6 hours    | Economy Plus / Premium Economy      | Business (with `Department Manager` approval) |

Rail travel under 4 hours is booked in standard class. Rail travel of 4
hours or more may be booked in first class when the fare difference is
under $75.

### 2.1 Upgrades `NWP-POL-002-02.01`

Employees may use personal loyalty-program upgrades at their own expense
or using personal points; Northwind Prairie Holdings, Inc. does not
reimburse the cash cost of a class-of-service upgrade beyond what this
section authorizes.

## 3. Advance Booking Requirement `NWP-POL-002-03`

Air and rail travel must be booked at least 14 days in advance whenever
the travel need is known that far ahead. Bookings made fewer than 14 days
in advance require a documented business justification in the expense
report notes field.

## 4. Change and Cancellation Fees `NWP-POL-002-04`

Change and cancellation fees are reimbursable when the change is required
by a legitimate business need (rescheduled client meeting, illness,
weather disruption). Change fees resulting from personal preference are
not reimbursable and must be paid out of pocket.
