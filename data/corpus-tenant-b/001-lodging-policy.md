## 1. Purpose and Scope `RVL-POL-001-01`

This policy sets nightly hotel caps for employees of Rival Corp
Holdings, Inc. traveling on approved business activity. Caps vary by
city tier to reflect real differences in market lodging cost.

## 2. Nightly Hotel Caps by City Tier `RVL-POL-001-02`

| City tier | Example markets (synthetic) | Nightly cap (USD) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 (major metro) | Fictional cities: Rivermarch, Eastgate Crossing | $325 |
| Tier 2 (secondary metro) | Fictional cities: Millbrook Junction, Fenwood | $215 |
| Tier 3 (all other locations) | All other synthetic locations | $145 |

Nightly caps are inclusive of room rate and mandatory hotel fees, but
exclusive of taxes, which are separately reimbursable. How much you can
spend on a hotel room per night depends entirely on which city tier your
destination falls under.

### 2.1 Exceeding the Cap `RVL-POL-001-02.01`

Bookings exceeding the applicable nightly cap require prior written
approval from a manager. Conference-hosted-hotel bookings that exceed
the cap are pre-approved automatically when the hotel is the official
conference venue and the excess is under $40/night.

## 3. Extended Stays `RVL-POL-001-03`

For stays of 14 consecutive nights or longer at one location, employees
should book extended-stay or corporate-rate lodging where available,
which is often below the standard nightly cap.
